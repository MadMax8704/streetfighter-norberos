RAGDOLL MASTERS

PC:
← → / A D – move
↑ / W / Space – jump
J – punch  ·  K – kick
L – special (when meter is full)
Combos: ←/→ ×2 + J – Dash Smash · J J + K – Finisher
S + K – Sweep · S + J (in air) – Dive Bomb
J J J – Rush · K K – Spin Kick
Phone:
left joystick – move + jump
right buttons – punch / kick / special

down + PUNCH (in air) – Dive Bomb · PUNCH ×3 – Rush · KICK ×2 – Spin Kick
Tip: 6 combo moves – dash smash, finisher, sweep, dive bomb, rush & spin kick · new enemy types from wave 2, BOSS every 5th wave!
